The Estimator Gap List

Twenty-five specialty trade subcontractors ranked on one thesis: they are winning more invitations to tender than their estimating desk can price. UK first, then Ireland, the Netherlands and Denmark.

Compiled 20 Aug 2026 Method: public web search only Every claim carries its source Gaps are flagged, not filled

Read this before you use the list

This research ran in a sandbox where direct page fetching was blocked by network policy — I could query a search index but could not open company websites, Companies House filings, LinkedIn, or job boards. Every fact below comes from a search-engine result or its snippet summary of the page named in the source column. Practical consequences:

  • Job-posting dates are mostly unverified. I can show that a vacancy exists or existed on a named page; I could not read most posting dates, so "last 6 months" is unconfirmed except where stated.
  • LinkedIn URLs appear only where a search result surfaced one. I have not invented profile URLs, and most rows say so.
  • Revenue and headcount are labelled by source quality. Trade-press figures drawn from filed accounts are marked verified; data-broker figures (RocketReach, ZoomInfo, Pomanda) are marked as broker claims and should be re-checked at Companies House / CVR / KVK before you spend money on them.
  • Denmark and Sweden are thin. I could not verify a single in-band Danish or Swedish independent to this standard. Rather than pad the list with plausible names, I have given you the exact portals and search strings to work those two markets yourself.

Top tier, and why

Kimpton — Liverpool

The only firm here that demonstrably pays market rates to buy estimating capacity: it lifted a lead mechanical estimator and four colleagues out of a competitor in one move. A business that solves the problem that way has already priced the problem, which makes your discount to a salaried hire an easy comparison. £25m and 55 people means one decision-maker, and the 2024 MBO team owns the P&L outright.

Evans Group — South Wales

Turnover doubled in a single year on a fixed 120-person base, with an order book stretching into 2027. That is the exact arithmetic of a firm declining tenders: the work is already sold, so every incremental invitation competes with delivery for the same pre-construction hours.

Arc-Tech (Scotland) — Glasgow

Ten years from founding to £40m. Firms compounding at this rate hire estimators last, because founders keep pricing personally for far longer than they should. Public-sector framework work in Scotland also means high tender volume at moderate ticket size — the profile where AI-assisted pricing pays back fastest.

Rockwell Engineering — Co. Cork

A published plan to go from €40m to €100m by 2030 under a brand-new MD. New MDs buy tools their predecessors would not, and a 2.5× revenue target cannot be met at current bid throughput. Best Irish entry point on this list.

Where these firms come from — work these directly

Highest yield: regional accounts coverage

Insider Media (regional editions), Construction Wave, Construction Enquirer, Construction News, Place Midlands, Project Scotland, East Midlands Business Link, Express & Star, Yorkshire Times, Wales 247, Business News Wales. These publish filed-accounts stories naming turnover, headcount, order book and directors in one paragraph — the single best source for this profile.

Framework portals (recent-win signal)

Pagabo (£545m M&E Solutions framework, 47 suppliers, 74% SMEs), Fusion21 (£820m Heating, Renewables & Electrical, 80 firms, 84% SMEs; £800m Building Safety & Compliance), LHC Procurement Group (regional lots, SME-friendly), NHS SBS PS-Works, Procure Partnerships, Find a Tender, Contracts Finder, Stotles, BidStats.

Trade bodies and directories

BESA and its awards (turnover-banded categories), ECA — whose award classes literally segment members at £5.1–15m, £15.1–45m and £45m+, which maps onto your band better than any database — FIA, BAFSA, Construction Specialists directory, The Construction Index. In NL: Techniek Nederland members and bouwberichten.nl. In DK: TEKNIQ Arbejdsgiverne and installator.dk. In SE: Installatörsföretagen's "hitta installatör", plus VVS-Forum and Elinstallatören's annual turnover rankings.

Company data (verify size here)

Companies House, Endole, Northdata, Pomanda (UK/IE); KVK and companyinfo.nl (NL); CVR and proff.dk (DK); allabolag.se (SE). Treat RocketReach, ZoomInfo and Crunchbase figures as leads only — several contradicted the trade press in this research.

Job boards where employers post direct

Company careers pages first (CMB, A. de Jong, Klomp, van den Buijs and Hollander all advertise estimating roles themselves). Then Find a Job (DWP), Indeed, Totaljobs, Jobsite, CV-Library, CIBSE Journal Jobs; Irish Jobs and Breagh Recruitment in Ireland; Indeed.nl and Glassdoor.nl; Indeed.dk and Jooble.dk; Indeed.se and Jooble.se.

Specialist recruiters as a proxy signal

When a firm has retained Contract Scotland, Moston, Building Careers UK, Ernest Gordon, Strong Recruitment or Breagh for an estimator, it has already accepted a five-figure fee to solve this. Recruiter adverts hide the client name, but the region-plus-project-size description usually identifies the firm within two or three candidates.

What I could not do, and what it would take

  • Denmark and Sweden. No in-band independent verified. Danish and Swedish company-level coverage sits behind Proff, CVR, allabolag and the trade weeklies, none of which I could open. A single afternoon with allabolag.se filtered on SNI 43.21/43.22 at SEK 50–500m, and CVR filtered on branchekode 432100/432200 at 30–250 employees, would produce the equivalent list for both markets.
  • Ireland below €50m. Irish trade press covers the data-centre giants; the €5–50m tier is largely invisible to search. The CIF membership directory and Breagh Recruitment's live estimator briefs are the way in.
  • Vacancy dates. Confirming "posted in the last 6 months" needs the job pages themselves. Each firm in Table 2 can be settled in one page-load.
  • Named commercial directors. Fourteen of the twenty-five have no named contact here. LinkedIn Sales Navigator filtered to the company plus "estimating"/"commercial"/"pre-construction" closes that gap far faster than public search does.
